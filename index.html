    My Story

    As a Volunteer IT Support Technician at the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) Uganda, I contributed directly to the Mass Renewal Project in my home district by installing software that improved biometric accuracy and helping resolve critical system errors. This field experience gave me practical exposure to managing private networks, supporting internal infrastructure, and enforcing data security compliance under real-world pressure.

    I graduated with a First Class Bachelor of Computer Applications degree from Amity University, anchoring my technical capabilities in strong computing fundamentals. Since then I have channeled my passion for defensive and offensive security into continuous execution: earning the TryHackMe SOC Level 1 certification, deploying multi-node enterprise security labs with Wazuh SIEM, completing many rooms on CyberTalents, and passing rigorous backend programming assessments.

    Whether I am writing custom automation scripts in Python, investigating spearphishing payloads, or optimizing data pipelines, my goal remains steadfast: building secure, dependable systems that protect people and data.
